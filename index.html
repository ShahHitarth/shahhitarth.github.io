About me

Where a spec becomes silicon.

I'm a third-year Electronics & Communication Engineering student at Vishwakarma Government Engineering College, Ahmedabad, specialising in ASIC and physical design.

I'm most at home where a design meets its constraints — synthesis, timing, floorplans and place-and-route. I built that foundation on industry tools during a summer internship, and I keep extending it on my own with open-source flows and Verilog projects.

Before chips, I built things by hand: ultrasonic radars, op-amp test kits, microcontroller security systems and a solar-powered smart home. That habit of making something work end to end is what I bring to a team — along with a placed fourth at NASA Space Apps, a nanosatellite payload concept presented to SAC ISRO scientists, and a patent application.
